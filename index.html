GINGA

Draw walls on a chart of the night sky until it falls apart into galaxies. Each holds one star and looks the same after a half turn about it. The app teaches you how.

Not in the App Store yet. It will run on iPhone and iPad.

Learning is part of the game

Four rule steps

One move each, on a chart where the new rule settles it and nothing else does.

Seven ideas

From stars on a line to galaxies that can't reach a cell. Each idea is worked once in front of you, then you try two.

Hints in stages

Ask for as much as you need: the area, then the idea's name, then the reasoning drawn over your chart.

Screens

The charts

Tonight's chart

A new 9×9 every day, identical for all players: gentle on Monday, hard on Sunday.

Darker skies

Dusk, Twilight, Night, Midnight and Deep Sky across 7×7, 9×9 and 11×11. The hardest idea a chart needs sets its darkness.

One answer

The app finishes each chart with its own seven ideas before it reaches you, so it can be solved without a guess.

What it costs

Free: the rule steps, five lessons, the 7×7 and 9×9 charts and the daily chart. The Whole Sky is a single purchase that adds the last two lessons, the 11×11 chart and the staged hints. No ads, no data collected, no subscription.
