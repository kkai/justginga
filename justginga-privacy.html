Privacy

Just Ginga, updated 3 October 2026

Nothing about you leaves your device through Just Ginga. You never create an account, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics. There is no server behind it.

Your data

The app saves your progress through the lessons, your seals and best times, your daily streak, your settings and any chart you have not finished. All of this sits in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad, and it is gone when you delete the app.

The purchase

The Whole Sky is bought once through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment. To know whether to unlock it, the app asks Apple if you own it and remembers the answer locally. Your card details and Apple account stay with Apple.

Connections

Every chart is made by the app itself, offline. It goes online for App Store requests only: buying, restoring, and confirming ownership.

Children

Players of every age are welcome. The app keeps no record of who plays, so it keeps none about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text goes on this page with a new date at the top.
